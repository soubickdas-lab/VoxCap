====================================================================
Ai33.Pro / OpenSpeaker  —  TTS TAG CHEAT SHEET
Engine: ElevenLabs v3   |   Tags = app ke apne Tags menu se
====================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PAUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
[pause]                 short natural pause
                        She stopped. [pause] Then she ran.

[long_pause]            longer dramatic pause
                        And the killer is [long_pause] her brother.

<break time="1.5s"/>    force pause, exact length
                        Think about it. <break time="3s"/> Time is up.

NOTE: <break> hi ek SSML tag hai jo yahan chalta hai.
      Max 3 seconds per tag. 15-sec timer = <break time="3s"/> x 5.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SPEED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
[speed_very_slow]       slowest      -> big reveal, final warning
[speed_slow]            slower       -> question line, clue explanation
[speed_fast]            faster       -> setup, list of differences
[speed_very_fast]       fastest      -> rapid-fire intro, hype

NOTE: right panel ka Speed slider (1.00 = normal) poori clip pe lagta hai;
      tags sirf us hisse pe.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SOUND EFFECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
[cough]                 [cough]ahem, text...
[laughter]              [laughter]haha, text...
[crying]                [crying]boohoo, text...
[screaming]             [screaming]ahh, text...
[burping]               [burping]burp, text...
[humming]               [humming]hmm, text...
[sigh]                  [sigh]uh, text...
[sniff]                 [sniff]sniff, text...
[sneeze]                [sneeze]achoo, text...

PATTERN: tag -> matching sound word -> comma -> aapki line.
         Woh word na ho to effect aksar skip ho jaata hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EMOTION  (Experimental menu)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
[affection]     [amusement]     [anger]         [awe]
[confusion]     [contentment]   [determination] [disgust]
[elation]       [enthusiasm]    [fear]          [longing]
[pride]         [relief]        [sadness]       [surprise]

Tag ko us sentence ke thik pehle rakho. Ek-do sentence baad effect
fade ho jaata hai -> lambe passage me dobara tag karo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DELIVERY STYLE  (Experimental menu)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
[whispering]            whispered delivery
[shouting]              shouted delivery
[singing]               sung delivery
[pitch_low]             lower voice pitch
[pitch_high]            higher voice pitch
[expressive_high]       more expressive / more emotion
[expressive_low]        less expressive / flat, neutral

--------------------------------------------------------------------
6. YE YAHAN NAHI CHALTE  (bol ke sunaa dega)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
<speak>   <p>   <s>   <prosody>   <emphasis>
<say-as>  <sub> <phoneme>  <audio>  <voice>  <lang>  <mark>

Sirf <break time="..."/> bachta hai. Baaki sab [square bracket] tag ho.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PRACTICAL TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ek time pe ek tag sabse reliable. 2-3 stack karoge to delivery random.
- Tag text ke bilkul pehle — beech me space ya line break nahi.
- Emotion tags "Experimental" hain. Take kharab lage to pehle regenerate
  karo, script badalne se pehle.
- Har jagah chhote break mat daalo — zyada breaks se performance flat.
- Numbers words me likho (zero four, na ki 04).
- Lamba script chunks me render karo — tone consistent, credits control me.
- Subtitle chahiye to "Export transcript" on kar do.
