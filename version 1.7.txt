==========================================
  VoxCap — Version 1.7
==========================================

Ye file batati hai ki aapke paas kaunsa version hai.
Latest version yahan se milta hai:
https://github.com/soubickdas-lab/VoxCap/releases/latest/download/VoxCap-share.zip
Update karne ke liye bas update.bat double-click karo.

------------------------------------------
FEATURES
------------------------------------------
- Audio/Video → CapCut-style SRT captions (fully offline, Whisper large-v3)
- Meaningful line breaks — har caption line ka apna matlab banta hai
  (comma, connector words, pause par break; kabhi mid-phrase cut nahi)
- AI se audio banao (ai33.pro) - upload ki jagah text likho, audio ban jaati
  hai aur usi se SRT bhi. Dono modes me chalta hai
- SRT Matcher mode — apna script/beats do, har line audio se time-match hoti hai
- Drop karte hi auto-transcribe, SRT auto-download + live preview
- Coverage % — verify karo ki pura text captions me aaya (100% hona chahiye)
- Modern player + real-time highlight se audio-SRT sync check
- Hindi, English + 99 languages, auto-detect
- Precise word timestamps (GPU par accurate mode)
- GPU na ho to CPU par medium model se kaam chalta hai
- Badi files (GB+) chunked upload se — Cloudflare 100MB limit ka fix
- Start / Stop buttons — Stop turant pura process band karta hai
- Caption length slider (40–90 characters)

  Delivery, Pause, Sound) - click karo, cursor par lag jaata hai

v1.6 (12 Aug 2026)  — ACCURACY UPDATE
- BUG FIX (bada): jin segments ke word-timestamps nahi bante the, unka
  POORA text chup-chaap gayab ho jata tha — isi se SRT lines miss ho rahi
  thi. Ab aise case me segment ka text uske time span me baant diya jata
  hai. Ek bhi line ab drop nahi hoti
- Dheemi awaaz ab nahi katati: VAD kam aggressive kiya (threshold 0.5 se
  0.25, zyada padding) — halki/soft speech bhi transcribe hoti hai
- Audio skip hona band: condition_on_previous_text=False — ek kharab
  segment ke baad model bhatak ke aage ka audio chhod nahi deta
- no_speech / log_prob thresholds dheele — halke segments "silence" maan
  ke discard nahi hote
- Timestamps sanitize — ulte/overlapping time se captions kharab nahi hote
- UI me ab bade pills: total SRT lines, coverage %, language, duration, words
- Coverage %: auto mode me "text captured" (100% = kuch miss nahi),
  matcher mode me "lines audio se matched"
